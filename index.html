Celestiapedia Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

In short: Celestiapedia has no accounts, no ads and no analytics. We do not collect your name, email address or any other personal information, and we do not have a server that stores anything about you. Everything the app remembers (like your reminders and your chosen location) stays on your own phone.

Who we are

Celestiapedia is an Android app about space, made by Gérard du Plessis in Pretoria, South Africa. In this policy, "we" and "the app" mean Celestiapedia and its developer. You can reach us at gerard.duplessis@gmail.com.

For children and parents

Celestiapedia is made for curious people of all ages, including children aged 8 and up. The app does not ask anyone for their name, age, email address or any other personal information, and it has no sign-up, chat or social features.

The app has links to other websites, for example photo credits, space news stories and live streams of rocket launches. We do not control those websites. To keep children safe, a link opens only after a quick question meant for a grown-up. Grown-ups can choose to stay unlocked for 30 days and can switch this off again on the Credits page. This is a safety speed bump, not an age check, so parents are still the best judge of what their children open.

What stays on your phone

The app saves a few things on your phone only. We never receive them:

  • Your chosen location for the Tonight's Sky card (from your phone's location, or from a city you pick), used only on your phone to work out your sky.
  • Launch reminders you set.
  • Your favourites, your red night-mode setting and other app settings.
  • Whether you have seen the welcome note, and whether grown-up mode is switched on.
  • Saved copies of the latest launches, news, asteroids, space weather and pictures, so the app can show something when you are offline.

Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data in your phone's settings, removes all of this. Android's own backup feature may copy an app's saved data to your own Google account, depending on your phone's settings. That copy belongs to you, and we cannot see it.

Permissions the app asks for

  • Internet to load launches, news, space weather and pictures.
  • Location (approximate or precise), only if you tap "Use my location". Your location is used on your phone to work out the Moon, planets and satellite passes for where you are. It is not sent to us or put in any request. You can use the app fully by picking a city instead, and you can turn the permission off at any time in your phone's settings.
  • Notifications and alarms to deliver the launch reminders you ask for. These reminders are created on your phone. We do not send notifications from a server.

Services the app connects to

To show live information, the app contacts the public services below directly from your phone. Like any website or app, they can see your phone's internet (IP) address and the basic technical details your phone sends with every request. We do not send them your name, your location or any identifier of ours, and each service has its own privacy policy.

The app asks for new information only now and then (for example launches about once an hour, news about every half hour), and keeps a saved copy so it does not ask more often than needed.

Links to other websites

When a grown-up opens a link, the app opens the website in your phone's browser or another app. That website then has its own privacy rules. For example, the live streams open on the video site that hosts them. We are not responsible for what other websites do with your information.

What we do not do

  • We do not show ads or use advertising IDs.
  • We do not use analytics, tracking or crash-reporting tools.
  • We do not create accounts or ask you to sign up.
  • We do not sell, rent or share personal information, because we do not collect any.

Your choices

  • Turn off location or notifications at any time in your phone's settings.
  • Use a city instead of your phone's location.
  • Clear the app's data or uninstall it to remove everything saved on your phone.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects your privacy, we will update this page and the date at the top.
